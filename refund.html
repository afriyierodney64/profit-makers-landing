Refund Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Refund Policy explains when you can get a refund or credit for purchases from Profit Makers. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

In short: predictions are digital content, so once a booking code has been revealed to you it cannot be refunded. If we cannot deliver, or a slip is cancelled before kickoff, you get your money back or credit.

1. Single premium slips

  • No refunds once revealed. Once a booking code or prediction has been revealed to you (on the website or Telegram), the purchase is final, whether the slip wins or loses.
  • Cancelled or voided before kickoff. If we cancel or void a slip before the first match kicks off, you will receive a full refund or account credit of the same value, at your choice.
  • Technical errors. If you were charged but a technical problem stopped us delivering your code, and we cannot fix it before kickoff, you will receive a full refund or credit.
  • Duplicate payments. If you were charged twice for the same item, the duplicate will be refunded.

A losing slip, a selection voided by your bookmaker after kickoff, or changed odds on the bookmaker's side are not grounds for a refund.

2. VIP plans

  • You can cancel your VIP plan at any time.
  • When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the week or month you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.
  • We do not give partial or pro-rata refunds for unused days.
  • If a technical fault on our side stops you accessing VIP content for a significant part of your paid period, contact us and we will extend your plan or give a fair credit.

3. How to request a refund or credit

Contact us within 7 days of the payment at hello@profitmakers.example or +233 00 000 0000. Please include:

  • Your name and the phone number or email used to pay
  • The Paystack payment reference (from your receipt or SMS)
  • The slip or plan purchased and a short description of the problem

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (Mobile Money wallet or card) through Paystack. Mobile Money refunds usually arrive within a few business days; card refunds can take up to 10 business days depending on your bank.

4. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank or mobile money provider; most issues can be resolved quickly. Accounts with fraudulent chargebacks may be suspended.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects any rights you have under Ghanaian consumer protection law that cannot be excluded.
