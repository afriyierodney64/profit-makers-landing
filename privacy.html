Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profit Makers ("we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you use our website, Telegram channels and services. We process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana.

1. Data we collect

  • Account and contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Telegram details: your Telegram username or ID, if you link Telegram to receive codes.
  • Purchase records: what you bought, the amount, date, and the Paystack payment reference and status.
  • Technical data: basic device, browser and usage information (such as IP address and pages visited) used to keep the site secure and working.

2. Payments

All payments are handled by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. Your card number, CVV and Mobile Money PIN are entered on Paystack's secure checkout. We never see or store your full card details or PINs. We only receive confirmation of the payment, the reference and limited details such as the payment channel and the last digits of a card.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and deliver the predictions you buy
  • To send booking codes, VIP updates and renewal reminders by SMS, email or Telegram
  • To process payments, refunds and customer support requests
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and resale of paid content
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations
  • To send marketing messages, only where you have agreed; you can opt out at any time

4. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service, such as Paystack (payments), our hosting provider, SMS/email providers and Telegram (message delivery), and with authorities where required by law. These providers may process data outside Ghana; where they do, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active and purchase records for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes (typically up to 6 years). You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please keep your login details private.

7. Your rights

Under Act 843 you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its use for direct marketing, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

8. Children

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and will delete such data if we become aware of it.

9. Cookies

We use only essential cookies and similar storage needed to keep you logged in and the site working. If we add analytics cookies in future, we will update this policy.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@profitmakers.example or +233 00 000 0000.
