FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly am I paying for?

You pay for access to our football analysis and predictions, delivered as a bookmaker booking code (e.g. SportyBet, Betway, 1xBet) plus the picks behind it. You place any bet yourself with your bookmaker.

Do you guarantee wins?

No. Nobody can guarantee football results. Our predictions are data-driven opinions, and we publish our win/loss record so you can judge our performance yourself.

How do I pay?

All payments go through Paystack. You can pay with Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) or a Visa/Mastercard card. We never see or store your card details.

How fast do I get my booking code?

Instantly. As soon as Paystack confirms your payment, the code unlocks on your account page and is sent to you on Telegram.

Can I get a refund?

Because predictions are digital content, payments are non-refundable once a booking code has been revealed. If a slip is cancelled or voided before kickoff, or a technical problem stops us delivering your code, we will refund you or give you credit. See our Refund Policy.

Can I cancel my VIP plan?

Yes, any time. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for, and you will not be charged again. We do not give partial refunds for unused days.

Do you take bets or hold my money?

No. Profit Makers is not a bookmaker. We do not accept bets, hold stakes, or pay out winnings. We only sell analysis and predictions.
